Glossary

The eight terms the rest of the site assumes. Every one of them is measured on a page you can open — nothing here is a word we made up to avoid showing the number behind it.

The Call
Our locked pre-tip projection — spread, total, win chance.
Line Impact
How many points a player moves the spread at her projected minutes.
Deserved record
The record a team's schedule-adjusted play has earned, coin flips removed.
Leverage
How much a game can swing the playoff picture, measured in probability.
Coverage
How often results land inside our stated range — said 90, should be 90.
Beat rate
The share of games our call was closer than the closing line.
Confirmed vs projected
Whether a lineup is announced, or our model's expectation.
The breakdown
The visible inputs that sum exactly to every call.

Where each one is measured: Our Accuracy grades the calls and reports coverage and beat rate, the Error Map says where we missed, Injuries is where confirmed and projected lineups are marked, and Learn walks through the reasoning from the ground up.

The Hardwood is an independent WNBA prediction and simulation project. Not affiliated with the WNBA. Every projection on this site is locked before tip-off and graded the next morning — the record is public.
