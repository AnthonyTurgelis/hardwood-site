Our call, graded

Before tip we published Washington Mystics by 6.5 on the home margin, and a 71% win probability for Washington Mystics. Washington Mystics won by 8, so our number missed by 1.5 points (under-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-11.2 to +22.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 1.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 2.5 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 174.0 total points and the game produced 176, a 2.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
