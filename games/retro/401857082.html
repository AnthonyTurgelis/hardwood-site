Our call, graded

Before tip we published Phoenix Mercury by 8.9 on the home margin, and a 64% win probability for Phoenix Mercury. Phoenix Mercury won by 9, so our number missed by 0.1 points (under-favoring Phoenix Mercury). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-12.1 to +23.6 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 1.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 0.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 170.2 total points and the game produced 135, a 35.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
