Our call, graded

Before tip we published Las Vegas Aces by 10.4 on the home margin, and a 81% win probability for Las Vegas Aces. Las Vegas Aces won by 15, so our number missed by 4.6 points (under-favoring Las Vegas Aces). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-7.2 to +26.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 23.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 18.4 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 176.7 total points and the game produced 181, a 4.3-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
