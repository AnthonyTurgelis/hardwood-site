Our call, graded

Before tip we published New York Liberty by 8.8 on the home margin, and a 77% win probability for New York Liberty. New York Liberty won by 12, so our number missed by 3.2 points (under-favoring New York Liberty). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-8.8 to +24.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 18.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 14.8 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 183.7 total points and the game produced 178, a 5.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
