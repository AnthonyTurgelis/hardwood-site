Our call, graded

Before tip we published Chicago Sky by 6.1 on the home margin, and a 70% win probability for Chicago Sky. Chicago Sky won by 6, so our number missed by 0.1 points (over-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-11.5 to +22.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 5.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.1 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 178.4 total points and the game produced 182, a 3.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
