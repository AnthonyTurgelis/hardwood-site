Our call, graded

Before tip we published New York Liberty by 2.4 on the home margin, and a 44% win probability for Toronto Tempo. Toronto Tempo won by 2, so our number missed by 4.4 points (under-favoring Toronto Tempo). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-23.4 to +12.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 16.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 11.6 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 169.3 total points and the game produced 184, a 14.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
