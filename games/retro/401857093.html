Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 14.6 on the home margin, and a 89% win probability for Minnesota Lynx. Minnesota Lynx won by 7, so our number missed by 7.6 points (over-favoring Minnesota Lynx). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-3.0 to +30.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 10.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 17.6 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 175.0 total points and the game produced 193, a 18.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
