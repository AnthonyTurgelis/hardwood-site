Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 11.1 on the home margin, and a 81% win probability for Atlanta Dream. Atlanta Dream won by 12, so our number missed by 0.9 points (under-favoring Atlanta Dream). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-4.8 to +27.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 3.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 2.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 177.0 total points and the game produced 202, a 25.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
