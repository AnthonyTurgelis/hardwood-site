Our call, graded

Before tip we published Las Vegas Aces by 1.4 on the home margin, and a 45% win probability for Washington Mystics. Washington Mystics won by 1, so our number missed by 2.4 points (under-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-19.0 to +14.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 5.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 7.4 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 168.2 total points and the game produced 199, a 30.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
