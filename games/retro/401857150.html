Our call, graded

Splitting the miss: roughly 13.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 7.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 179.4 total points and the game produced 186, a 6.6-point miss. This grade is fixed.
