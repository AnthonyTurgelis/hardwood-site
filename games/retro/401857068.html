Our call, graded

Before tip we published Chicago Sky by 3.9 on the home margin, and a 54% win probability for Chicago Sky. Chicago Sky won by 5, so our number missed by 1.1 points (under-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-17.0 to +18.6 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 9.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 7.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 170.1 total points and the game produced 185, a 14.9-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
