Our call, graded

Before tip we published Washington Mystics by 4.5 on the home margin, and a 56% win probability for Washington Mystics. Portland Fire won by 19, so our number missed by 23.5 points (over-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-16.4 to +19.2 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 25.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.5 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 163.6 total points and the game produced 131, a 32.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
