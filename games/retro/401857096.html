Our call, graded

Before tip we published New York Liberty by 0.8 on the home margin, and a 47% win probability for Los Angeles Sparks. New York Liberty won by 4, so our number missed by 3.2 points (over-favoring Los Angeles Sparks). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-18.4 to +15.1 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 2.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.2 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 184.2 total points and the game produced 222, a 37.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
