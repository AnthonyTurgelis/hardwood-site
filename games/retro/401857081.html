Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 11.2 on the home margin, and a 68% win probability for Atlanta Dream. Atlanta Dream won by 2, so our number missed by 9.2 points (over-favoring Atlanta Dream). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-9.7 to +25.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 13.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 3.8 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 172.3 total points and the game produced 184, a 11.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
