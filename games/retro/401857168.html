Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 3.8 on the home margin, and a 38% win probability for Chicago Sky. Indiana Fever won by 23, so our number missed by 19.2 points (over-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-19.8 to +12.0 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 18.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 175.7 total points and the game produced 203, a 27.3-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
