Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 12.6 on the home margin, and a 86% win probability for Indiana Fever. Indiana Fever won by 35, so our number missed by 22.4 points (under-favoring Indiana Fever). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-5.1 to +28.5 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 29.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 6.6 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 171.1 total points and the game produced 211, a 39.9-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
