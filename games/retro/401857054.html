Our call, graded

Before tip we published Golden State Valkyries by 2.9 on the home margin, and a 43% win probability for Connecticut Sun. Golden State Valkyries won by 15, so our number missed by 12.1 points (over-favoring Connecticut Sun). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-23.9 to +11.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 16.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 3.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 169.8 total points and the game produced 143, a 26.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
