Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 0.8 on the home margin, and a 51% win probability for Dallas Wings. Dallas Wings won by 8, so our number missed by 7.2 points (under-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-20.2 to +15.4 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 2.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.2 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 176.0 total points and the game produced 172, a 4.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
