Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 8.5 on the home margin, and a 75% win probability for Dallas Wings. Dallas Wings won by 22, so our number missed by 13.5 points (under-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-7.5 to +24.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 23.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 9.5 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 176.4 total points and the game produced 162, a 14.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
