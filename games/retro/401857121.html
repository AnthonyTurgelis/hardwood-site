Our call, graded

Before tip we published Portland Fire by 2.9 on the home margin, and a 60% win probability for Portland Fire. Portland Fire won by 14, so our number missed by 11.1 points (under-favoring Portland Fire). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.1 to +18.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 16.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 4.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 175.1 total points and the game produced 180, a 4.9-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
