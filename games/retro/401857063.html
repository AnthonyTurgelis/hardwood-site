Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 1.2 on the home margin, and a 46% win probability for Las Vegas Aces. Indiana Fever won by 34, so our number missed by 32.8 points (over-favoring Las Vegas Aces). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-22.2 to +13.5 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 34.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 173.0 total points and the game produced 184, a 11.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
