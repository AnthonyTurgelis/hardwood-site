Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 5.0 on the home margin, and a 67% win probability for Indiana Fever. Las Vegas Aces won by 2, so our number missed by 7.0 points (over-favoring Indiana Fever). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-11.0 to +20.8 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 13.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 20.0 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 184.4 total points and the game produced 170, a 14.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
