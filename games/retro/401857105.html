Our call, graded

Before tip we published Las Vegas Aces by 1.7 on the home margin, and a 44% win probability for Chicago Sky. Chicago Sky won by 1, so our number missed by 2.7 points (under-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-19.4 to +14.2 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 2.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 4.7 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 181.4 total points and the game produced 167, a 14.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
