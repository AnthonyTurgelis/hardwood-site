Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 6.2 on the home margin, and a 69% win probability for Indiana Fever. Indiana Fever won by 11, so our number missed by 4.8 points (under-favoring Indiana Fever). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-9.8 to +22.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 16.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 11.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 182.4 total points and the game produced 185, a 2.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
