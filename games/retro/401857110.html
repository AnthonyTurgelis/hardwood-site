Our call, graded

Before tip we published Golden State Valkyries by 11.5 on the home margin, and a 84% win probability for Golden State Valkyries. Golden State Valkyries won by 17, so our number missed by 5.5 points (under-favoring Golden State Valkyries). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-6.2 to +27.4 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 15.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 9.5 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 176.8 total points and the game produced 175, a 1.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
