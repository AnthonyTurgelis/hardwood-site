Our call, graded

Before tip we published Toronto Tempo by 3.6 on the home margin, and a 61% win probability for Toronto Tempo. Toronto Tempo won by 3, so our number missed by 0.6 points (over-favoring Toronto Tempo). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-12.4 to +19.4 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 2.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 2.6 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 174.7 total points and the game produced 161, a 13.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
