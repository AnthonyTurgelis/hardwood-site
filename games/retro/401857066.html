Our call, graded

Before tip we published Connecticut Sun by 0.9 on the home margin, and a 50% win probability for Connecticut Sun. Connecticut Sun won by 3, so our number missed by 2.1 points (under-favoring Connecticut Sun). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-20.1 to +15.6 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 1.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 3.1 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 166.8 total points and the game produced 177, a 10.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
