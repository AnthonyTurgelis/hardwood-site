Our call, graded

Before tip we published New York Liberty by 4.9 on the home margin, and a 35% win probability for Connecticut Sun. New York Liberty won by 7, so our number missed by 2.1 points (over-favoring Connecticut Sun). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-20.8 to +11.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 19.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 16.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 180.6 total points and the game produced 157, a 23.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
