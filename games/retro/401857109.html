Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 10.3 on the home margin, and a 81% win probability for Dallas Wings. Dallas Wings won by 20, so our number missed by 9.7 points (under-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-7.3 to +26.2 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 16.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 6.3 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.9 total points and the game produced 146, a 35.9-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
