Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 7.8 on the home margin, and a 37% win probability for Seattle Storm. Minnesota Lynx won by 3, so our number missed by 4.8 points (under-favoring Seattle Storm). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-28.8 to +6.8 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 5.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 0.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 172.3 total points and the game produced 207, a 34.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
