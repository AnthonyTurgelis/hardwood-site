Our call, graded

Before tip we published Golden State Valkyries by 2.1 on the home margin, and a 43% win probability for Phoenix Mercury. Phoenix Mercury won by 2, so our number missed by 4.1 points (under-favoring Phoenix Mercury). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-19.7 to +13.8 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 6.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 182.0 total points and the game produced 180, a 2.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
