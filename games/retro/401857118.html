Our call, graded

Before tip we published Chicago Sky by 2.7 on the home margin, and a 59% win probability for Chicago Sky. Chicago Sky won by 7, so our number missed by 4.3 points (under-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.3 to +18.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 8.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 3.7 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 184.6 total points and the game produced 183, a 1.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
