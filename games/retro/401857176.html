Our call, graded

Before tip we published Golden State Valkyries by 6.7 on the home margin, and a 30% win probability for Connecticut Sun. Golden State Valkyries won by 25, so our number missed by 18.3 points (over-favoring Connecticut Sun). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-22.7 to +9.1 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 36.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 17.7 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 174.1 total points and the game produced 153, a 21.1-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
