Our call, graded

Before tip we published Washington Mystics by 2.2 on the home margin, and a 57% win probability for Washington Mystics. Washington Mystics won by 20, so our number missed by 17.8 points (under-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.8 to +18.0 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 11.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 6.8 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 176.5 total points and the game produced 170, a 6.5-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
