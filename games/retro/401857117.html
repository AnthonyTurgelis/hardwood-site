Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 0.4 on the home margin, and a 49% win probability for Washington Mystics. Washington Mystics won by 4, so our number missed by 4.4 points (under-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-16.4 to +15.4 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 1.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.4 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 179.9 total points and the game produced 188, a 8.1-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
