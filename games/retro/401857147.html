Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 1.4 on the home margin, and a 45% win probability for Las Vegas Aces. Minnesota Lynx won by 5, so our number missed by 3.6 points (over-favoring Las Vegas Aces). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-17.4 to +14.4 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 9.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.4 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 180.7 total points and the game produced 179, a 1.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
