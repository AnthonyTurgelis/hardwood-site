Our call, graded

Before tip we published Chicago Sky by 2.1 on the home margin, and a 57% win probability for Chicago Sky. Phoenix Mercury won by 5, so our number missed by 7.1 points (over-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-15.6 to +17.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 4.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 3.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.2 total points and the game produced 207, a 25.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
