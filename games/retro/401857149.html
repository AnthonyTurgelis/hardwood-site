Our call, graded

Before tip we published Phoenix Mercury by 7.3 on the home margin, and a 72% win probability for Phoenix Mercury. Portland Fire won by 3, so our number missed by 10.3 points (over-favoring Phoenix Mercury). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-8.7 to +23.1 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 5.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.3 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 174.7 total points and the game produced 173, a 1.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
