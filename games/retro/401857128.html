Our call, graded

Before tip we published New York Liberty by 2.5 on the home margin, and a 58% win probability for New York Liberty. New York Liberty won by 40, so our number missed by 37.5 points (under-favoring New York Liberty). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.4 to +18.4 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 22.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 15.5 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.6 total points and the game produced 182, a 0.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
