Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 9.2 on the home margin, and a 21% win probability for Portland Fire. Minnesota Lynx won by 4, so our number missed by 5.2 points (under-favoring Portland Fire). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-25.2 to +6.6 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 0.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.2 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 180.8 total points and the game produced 166, a 14.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
