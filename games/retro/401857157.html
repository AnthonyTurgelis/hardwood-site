Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 0.3 on the home margin, and a 49% win probability for Golden State Valkyries. Minnesota Lynx won by 11, so our number missed by 10.7 points (over-favoring Golden State Valkyries). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-16.2 to +15.6 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 27.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 16.3 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 170.9 total points and the game produced 143, a 27.9-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
