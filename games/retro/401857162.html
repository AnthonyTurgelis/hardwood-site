Our call, graded

Before tip we published Golden State Valkyries by 3.2 on the home margin, and a 40% win probability for Chicago Sky. Chicago Sky won by 3, so our number missed by 6.2 points (under-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-19.1 to +12.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 16.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 9.8 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 169.5 total points and the game produced 143, a 26.5-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
