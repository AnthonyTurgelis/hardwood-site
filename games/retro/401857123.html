Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 2.0 on the home margin, and a 43% win probability for Washington Mystics. Washington Mystics won by 5, so our number missed by 7.0 points (under-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-18.0 to +13.8 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 23.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 16.0 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 180.2 total points and the game produced 153, a 27.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
