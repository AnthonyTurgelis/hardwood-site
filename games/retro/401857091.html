Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 3.2 on the home margin, and a 39% win probability for Portland Fire. Dallas Wings won by 4, so our number missed by 0.8 points (over-favoring Portland Fire). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-20.8 to +12.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 9.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 9.8 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 170.6 total points and the game produced 198, a 27.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
