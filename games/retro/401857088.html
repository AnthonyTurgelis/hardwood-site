Our call, graded

Before tip we published New York Liberty by 7.9 on the home margin, and a 75% win probability for New York Liberty. New York Liberty won by 1, so our number missed by 6.9 points (over-favoring New York Liberty). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-9.7 to +23.8 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 6.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 0.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 171.6 total points and the game produced 189, a 17.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
