Our call, graded

Before tip we published Las Vegas Aces by 3.1 on the home margin, and a 53% win probability for Las Vegas Aces. Las Vegas Aces won by 48, so our number missed by 44.9 points (under-favoring Las Vegas Aces). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-17.9 to +17.7 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 28.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 16.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 172.8 total points and the game produced 164, a 8.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
