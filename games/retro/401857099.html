Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 8.2 on the home margin, and a 24% win probability for Toronto Tempo. Minnesota Lynx won by 32, so our number missed by 23.8 points (over-favoring Toronto Tempo). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-25.8 to +7.7 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 6.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 17.8 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 178.3 total points and the game produced 176, a 2.3-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
