Our call, graded

Before tip we published Phoenix Mercury by 8.9 on the home margin, and a 64% win probability for Phoenix Mercury. Connecticut Sun won by 13, so our number missed by 21.9 points (over-favoring Phoenix Mercury). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-12.1 to +23.6 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 10.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 11.9 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 170.8 total points and the game produced 179, a 8.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
