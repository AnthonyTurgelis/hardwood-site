Our call, graded

Before tip we published Washington Mystics by 5.9 on the home margin, and a 68% win probability for Washington Mystics. Washington Mystics won by 11, so our number missed by 5.1 points (under-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-10.1 to +21.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 1.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 4.1 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 169.0 total points and the game produced 175, a 6.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
