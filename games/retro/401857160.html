Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 1.9 on the home margin, and a 44% win probability for Los Angeles Sparks. Atlanta Dream won by 36, so our number missed by 34.1 points (over-favoring Los Angeles Sparks). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-17.9 to +13.9 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 29.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.2 total points and the game produced 212, a 30.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
