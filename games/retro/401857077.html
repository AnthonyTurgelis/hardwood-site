Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 8.0 on the home margin, and a 63% win probability for Indiana Fever. Indiana Fever won by 20, so our number missed by 12.0 points (under-favoring Indiana Fever). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.0 to +22.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 13.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.0 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 175.2 total points and the game produced 196, a 20.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
