Our call, graded

Before tip we published Seattle Storm by 4.6 on the home margin, and a 64% win probability for Seattle Storm. Portland Fire won by 2, so our number missed by 6.6 points (over-favoring Seattle Storm). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-11.4 to +20.4 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 15.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.4 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 176.2 total points and the game produced 166, a 10.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
