Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 7.4 on the home margin, and a 75% win probability for Atlanta Dream. Atlanta Dream won by 14, so our number missed by 6.6 points (under-favoring Atlanta Dream). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-8.5 to +23.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 15.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.4 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 186.5 total points and the game produced 178, a 8.5-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
