Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 18.2 on the home margin, and a 78% win probability for Minnesota Lynx. Minnesota Lynx won by 8, so our number missed by 10.2 points (over-favoring Minnesota Lynx). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-2.8 to +32.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 6.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 16.2 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 169.5 total points and the game produced 194, a 24.5-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
