Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 7.9 on the home margin, and a 74% win probability for Minnesota Lynx. Minnesota Lynx won by 11, so our number missed by 3.1 points (under-favoring Minnesota Lynx). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-8.1 to +23.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 12.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.5 total points and the game produced 185, a 3.5-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
