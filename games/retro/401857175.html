Our call, graded

Before tip we published Phoenix Mercury by 4.3 on the home margin, and a 63% win probability for Phoenix Mercury. Washington Mystics won by 10, so our number missed by 14.3 points (over-favoring Phoenix Mercury). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-11.7 to +20.1 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 12.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 2.3 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 172.4 total points and the game produced 178, a 5.6-point miss. This grade is fixed.
