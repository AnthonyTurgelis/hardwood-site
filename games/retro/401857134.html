Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 5.7 on the home margin, and a 68% win probability for Indiana Fever. Indiana Fever won by 14, so our number missed by 8.3 points (under-favoring Indiana Fever). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-10.3 to +21.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 16.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 7.7 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 182.8 total points and the game produced 198, a 15.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
