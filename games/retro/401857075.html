Our call, graded

Before tip we published Los Angeles Sparks by 0.6 on the home margin, and a 49% win probability for Chicago Sky. Chicago Sky won by 14, so our number missed by 14.6 points (under-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-21.6 to +14.1 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 6.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.6 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 172.5 total points and the game produced 178, a 5.5-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
