Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 7.4 on the home margin, and a 26% win probability for Seattle Storm. Minnesota Lynx won by 10, so our number missed by 2.6 points (over-favoring Seattle Storm). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-25.0 to +8.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 1.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.6 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 171.6 total points and the game produced 162, a 9.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
