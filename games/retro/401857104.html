Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 5.4 on the home margin, and a 32% win probability for Portland Fire. Indiana Fever won by 14, so our number missed by 8.6 points (over-favoring Portland Fire). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-23.1 to +10.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 3.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 11.6 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 180.4 total points and the game produced 210, a 29.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
