Our call, graded

Before tip we published Golden State Valkyries by 11.5 on the home margin, and a 84% win probability for Golden State Valkyries. Golden State Valkyries won by 10, so our number missed by 1.5 points (over-favoring Golden State Valkyries). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-6.2 to +27.4 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 3.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.5 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 178.2 total points and the game produced 174, a 4.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
