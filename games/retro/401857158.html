Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 0.3 on the home margin, and a 51% win probability for Dallas Wings. Dallas Wings won by 6, so our number missed by 5.7 points (under-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-15.7 to +16.1 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 6.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 0.3 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.7 total points and the game produced 176, a 5.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
