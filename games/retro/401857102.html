Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 10.3 on the home margin, and a 81% win probability for Atlanta Dream. Atlanta Dream won by 9, so our number missed by 1.3 points (over-favoring Atlanta Dream). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-7.3 to +26.2 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 3.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 4.3 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 183.0 total points and the game produced 187, a 4.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
