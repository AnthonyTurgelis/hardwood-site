Our call, graded

Before tip we published Los Angeles Sparks by 8.1 on the home margin, and a 61% win probability for Los Angeles Sparks. Los Angeles Sparks won by 15, so our number missed by 6.9 points (under-favoring Los Angeles Sparks). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-12.9 to +22.8 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 19.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 12.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 173.0 total points and the game produced 189, a 16.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
