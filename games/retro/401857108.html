Our call, graded

Before tip we published Portland Fire by 0.4 on the home margin, and a 51% win probability for Portland Fire. Los Angeles Sparks won by 5, so our number missed by 5.4 points (over-favoring Portland Fire). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-17.2 to +16.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 11.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.6 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.7 total points and the game produced 207, a 25.3-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
