Our call, graded

Before tip we published New York Liberty by 1.3 on the home margin, and a 46% win probability for Chicago Sky. Chicago Sky won by 7, so our number missed by 8.3 points (under-favoring Chicago Sky). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-17.3 to +14.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 5.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 13.3 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 177.2 total points and the game produced 179, a 1.8-point miss. This grade is fixed.
