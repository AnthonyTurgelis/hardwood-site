Our call, graded

Before tip we published Los Angeles Sparks by 0.9 on the home margin, and a 47% win probability for Connecticut Sun. Connecticut Sun won by 8, so our number missed by 8.9 points (under-favoring Connecticut Sun). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-16.9 to +14.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 9.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 0.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 177.4 total points and the game produced 162, a 15.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
