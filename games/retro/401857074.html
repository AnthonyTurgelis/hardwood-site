Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 4.7 on the home margin, and a 42% win probability for Toronto Tempo. Atlanta Dream won by 19, so our number missed by 14.3 points (over-favoring Toronto Tempo). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-25.7 to +10.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 2.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 12.3 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 169.3 total points and the game produced 203, a 33.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
