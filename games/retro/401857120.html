Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 12.6 on the home margin, and a 87% win probability for Minnesota Lynx. Los Angeles Sparks won by 7, so our number missed by 19.6 points (over-favoring Minnesota Lynx). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-3.4 to +28.4 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 11.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.6 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 185.7 total points and the game produced 171, a 14.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
