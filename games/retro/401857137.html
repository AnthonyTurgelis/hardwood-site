Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 9.5 on the home margin, and a 80% win probability for Dallas Wings. Dallas Wings won by 6, so our number missed by 3.5 points (over-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-6.5 to +25.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 5.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.5 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 178.9 total points and the game produced 182, a 3.1-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
