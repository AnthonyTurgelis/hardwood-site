Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 7.3 on the home margin, and a 60% win probability for Atlanta Dream. Atlanta Dream won by 9, so our number missed by 1.7 points (under-favoring Atlanta Dream). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.7 to +22.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 4.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.7 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 175.8 total points and the game produced 193, a 17.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
