Our call, graded

Before tip we published Washington Mystics by 3.1 on the home margin, and a 60% win probability for Washington Mystics. Washington Mystics won by 10, so our number missed by 6.9 points (under-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-12.9 to +18.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 4.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 2.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 178.1 total points and the game produced 150, a 28.1-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
