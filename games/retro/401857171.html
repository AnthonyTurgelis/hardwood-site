Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 6.7 on the home margin, and a 70% win probability for Minnesota Lynx. Golden State Valkyries won by 14, so our number missed by 20.7 points (over-favoring Minnesota Lynx). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-9.2 to +22.6 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 10.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 10.7 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 175.4 total points and the game produced 146, a 29.4-point miss. This grade is fixed.
