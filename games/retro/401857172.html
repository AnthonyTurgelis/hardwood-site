Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 1.9 on the home margin, and a 44% win probability for Los Angeles Sparks. Atlanta Dream won by 7, so our number missed by 5.1 points (over-favoring Los Angeles Sparks). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-17.9 to +13.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 3.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 2.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 180.7 total points and the game produced 149, a 31.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
