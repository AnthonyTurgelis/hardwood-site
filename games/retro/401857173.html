Our call, graded

Before tip we published Chicago Sky by 0.3 on the home margin, and a 49% win probability for Connecticut Sun. Connecticut Sun won by 6, so our number missed by 6.3 points (under-favoring Connecticut Sun). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-16.3 to +15.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 3.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 3.3 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 172.9 total points and the game produced 168, a 4.9-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
