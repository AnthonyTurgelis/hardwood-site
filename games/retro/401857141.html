Our call, graded

Before tip we published New York Liberty by 7.2 on the home margin, and a 74% win probability for New York Liberty. New York Liberty won by 4, so our number missed by 3.2 points (over-favoring New York Liberty). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-8.8 to +23.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 12.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.8 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 187.4 total points and the game produced 166, a 21.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
