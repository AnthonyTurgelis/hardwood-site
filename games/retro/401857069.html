Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 12.0 on the home margin, and a 67% win probability for Minnesota Lynx. Minnesota Lynx won by 9, so our number missed by 3.0 points (over-favoring Minnesota Lynx). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-9.0 to +26.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 2.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.0 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 175.2 total points and the game produced 183, a 7.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
