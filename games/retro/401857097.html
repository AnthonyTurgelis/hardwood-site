Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 1.3 on the home margin, and a 54% win probability for Dallas Wings. Atlanta Dream won by 1, so our number missed by 2.3 points (over-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-16.3 to +17.2 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 10.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 12.3 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 185.4 total points and the game produced 163, a 22.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
