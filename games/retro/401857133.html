Our call, graded

Before tip we published Seattle Storm by 2.0 on the home margin, and a 56% win probability for Seattle Storm. Seattle Storm won by 9, so our number missed by 7.0 points (under-favoring Seattle Storm). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.9 to +17.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 7.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 0.0 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 178.0 total points and the game produced 185, a 7.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
