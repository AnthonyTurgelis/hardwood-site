Our call, graded

Before tip we published Golden State Valkyries by 2.6 on the home margin, and a 42% win probability for Los Angeles Sparks. Golden State Valkyries won by 6, so our number missed by 3.4 points (over-favoring Los Angeles Sparks). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-18.6 to +13.2 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 3.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 0.4 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.0 total points and the game produced 162, a 19.0-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
