Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 12.8 on the home margin, and a 70% win probability for Indiana Fever. Indiana Fever won by 3, so our number missed by 9.8 points (over-favoring Indiana Fever). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-8.2 to +27.5 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 8.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.8 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 172.3 total points and the game produced 217, a 44.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
