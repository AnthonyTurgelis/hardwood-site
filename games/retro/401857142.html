Our call, graded

Before tip we published Las Vegas Aces by 8.0 on the home margin, and a 76% win probability for Las Vegas Aces. Las Vegas Aces won by 7, so our number missed by 1.0 points (over-favoring Las Vegas Aces). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-8.0 to +23.8 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 8.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 7.0 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 178.2 total points and the game produced 159, a 19.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
