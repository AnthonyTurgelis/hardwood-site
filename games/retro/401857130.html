Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 9.1 on the home margin, and a 76% win probability for Minnesota Lynx. Minnesota Lynx won by 13, so our number missed by 3.9 points (under-favoring Minnesota Lynx). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-6.9 to +24.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 14.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 10.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 181.4 total points and the game produced 193, a 11.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
