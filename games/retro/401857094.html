Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 4.2 on the home margin, and a 36% win probability for Seattle Storm. Indiana Fever won by 10, so our number missed by 5.8 points (over-favoring Seattle Storm). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-21.8 to +11.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 19.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 13.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 180.1 total points and the game produced 200, a 19.9-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
