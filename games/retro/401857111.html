Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 4.4 on the home margin, and a 65% win probability for Atlanta Dream. Las Vegas Aces won by 22, so our number missed by 26.4 points (over-favoring Atlanta Dream). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.2 to +20.3 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 32.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 5.6 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 184.8 total points and the game produced 196, a 11.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
