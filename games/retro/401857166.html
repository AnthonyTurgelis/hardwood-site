Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 1.0 on the home margin, and a 47% win probability for Phoenix Mercury. Atlanta Dream won by 10, so our number missed by 9.0 points (over-favoring Phoenix Mercury). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-16.9 to +14.9 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 2.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 7.0 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 179.1 total points and the game produced 188, a 8.9-point miss. This grade is fixed.
