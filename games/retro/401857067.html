Our call, graded

Before tip we published Toronto Tempo by 2.7 on the home margin, and a 52% win probability for Toronto Tempo. Washington Mystics won by 17, so our number missed by 19.7 points (over-favoring Toronto Tempo). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-18.3 to +17.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 1.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 18.7 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 162.7 total points and the game produced 141, a 21.7-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
