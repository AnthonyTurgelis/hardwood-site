Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 11.1 on the home margin, and a 66% win probability for Atlanta Dream. Portland Fire won by 10, so our number missed by 21.1 points (over-favoring Atlanta Dream). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-9.9 to +25.8 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 29.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 7.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 171.4 total points and the game produced 194, a 22.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
