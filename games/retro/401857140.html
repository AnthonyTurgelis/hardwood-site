Our call, graded

Before tip we published Atlanta Dream by 6.0 on the home margin, and a 30% win probability for Connecticut Sun. Atlanta Dream won by 35, so our number missed by 29.0 points (over-favoring Connecticut Sun). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-22.0 to +9.8 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 25.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 4.0 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 183.5 total points and the game produced 173, a 10.5-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
