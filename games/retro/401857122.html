Our call, graded

Before tip we published Phoenix Mercury by 1.8 on the home margin, and a 44% win probability for Connecticut Sun. Connecticut Sun won by 3, so our number missed by 4.8 points (under-favoring Connecticut Sun). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-17.8 to +14.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 13.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 182.1 total points and the game produced 147, a 35.1-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
