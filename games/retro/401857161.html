Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 6.2 on the home margin, and a 31% win probability for Washington Mystics. Minnesota Lynx won by 10, so our number missed by 3.8 points (over-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-22.2 to +9.6 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 14.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 10.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 169.4 total points and the game produced 178, a 8.6-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
