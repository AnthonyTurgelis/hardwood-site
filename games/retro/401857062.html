Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 4.7 on the home margin, and a 56% win probability for Dallas Wings. Dallas Wings won by 5, so our number missed by 0.3 points (under-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-16.3 to +19.4 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 2.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.7 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 171.6 total points and the game produced 187, a 15.4-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
