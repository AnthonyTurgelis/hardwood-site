Our call, graded

Before tip we published Minnesota Lynx by 6.4 on the home margin, and a 71% win probability for Minnesota Lynx. Minnesota Lynx won by 8, so our number missed by 1.6 points (under-favoring Minnesota Lynx). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-11.2 to +22.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 0.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.6 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 184.5 total points and the game produced 208, a 23.5-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
