Our call, graded

Before tip we published Los Angeles Sparks by 2.7 on the home margin, and a 59% win probability for Los Angeles Sparks. Phoenix Mercury won by 4, so our number missed by 6.7 points (over-favoring Los Angeles Sparks). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-14.9 to +18.6 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 8.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.3 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 175.2 total points and the game produced 168, a 7.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
