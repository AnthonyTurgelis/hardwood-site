Our call, graded

Before tip we published Las Vegas Aces by 0.5 on the home margin, and a 49% win probability for Toronto Tempo. Las Vegas Aces won by 26, so our number missed by 25.5 points (over-favoring Toronto Tempo). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-21.5 to +14.2 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 34.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 8.5 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 167.8 total points and the game produced 192, a 24.2-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
