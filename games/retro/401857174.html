Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 9.9 on the home margin, and a 78% win probability for Dallas Wings. Dallas Wings won by 18, so our number missed by 8.1 points (under-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-6.1 to +25.7 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 26.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 17.9 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 174.3 total points and the game produced 174, a 0.3-point miss. This grade is fixed.
