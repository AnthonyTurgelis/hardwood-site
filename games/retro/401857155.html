Our call, graded

Before tip we published Las Vegas Aces by 2.8 on the home margin, and a 59% win probability for Las Vegas Aces. Atlanta Dream won by 15, so our number missed by 17.8 points (over-favoring Las Vegas Aces). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-13.2 to +18.6 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 4.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 13.8 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 179.2 total points and the game produced 179, a 0.2-point miss. This grade is fixed.
