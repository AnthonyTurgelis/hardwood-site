Our call, graded

Before tip we published Golden State Valkyries by 10.3 on the home margin, and a 67% win probability for Golden State Valkyries. Golden State Valkyries won by 5, so our number missed by 5.3 points (over-favoring Golden State Valkyries). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-10.7 to +25.0 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 1.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 6.3 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 167.8 total points and the game produced 143, a 24.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
