Our call, graded

Before tip we published Indiana Fever by 8.2 on the home margin, and a 62% win probability for Indiana Fever. Golden State Valkyries won by 13, so our number missed by 21.2 points (over-favoring Indiana Fever). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-12.8 to +22.9 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 14.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 7.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 172.1 total points and the game produced 163, a 9.1-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
