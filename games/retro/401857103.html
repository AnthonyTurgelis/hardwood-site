Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 0.6 on the home margin, and a 48% win probability for Washington Mystics. Washington Mystics won by 6, so our number missed by 6.6 points (under-favoring Washington Mystics). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-18.3 to +15.3 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 11.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 17.6 to process — so the dominant reason is model process (roles/minutes/matchup). On the scoring environment we projected 178.8 total points and the game produced 156, a 22.8-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
