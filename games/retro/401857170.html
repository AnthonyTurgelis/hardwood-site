Our call, graded

Before tip we published Las Vegas Aces by 4.2 on the home margin, and a 37% win probability for Toronto Tempo. Las Vegas Aces won by 10, so our number missed by 5.8 points (over-favoring Toronto Tempo). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-20.2 to +11.6 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 6.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 0.2 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 171.5 total points and the game produced 166, a 5.5-point miss. This grade is fixed.
