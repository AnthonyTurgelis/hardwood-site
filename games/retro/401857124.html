Our call, graded

Before tip we published Dallas Wings by 0.9 on the home margin, and a 53% win probability for Dallas Wings. Golden State Valkyries won by 18, so our number missed by 18.9 points (over-favoring Dallas Wings). That result landed outside the 80% interval we published with it (-15.1 to +16.7 on the home margin), which is a genuine tail outcome for a band that should miss one game in five. Splitting the miss: roughly 20.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 1.1 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 183.1 total points and the game produced 170, a 13.1-point miss. This grade is fixed. The number above was published before tip-off and is never restated after the fact; only its presentation changes.
