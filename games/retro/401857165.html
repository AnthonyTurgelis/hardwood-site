Our call, graded

Before tip we published Los Angeles Sparks by 7.4 on the home margin, and a 72% win probability for Los Angeles Sparks. Los Angeles Sparks won by 9, so our number missed by 1.6 points (under-favoring Los Angeles Sparks). That result landed inside the 80% interval we published with it (-8.6 to +23.2 on the home margin), which is the interval doing its job. Splitting the miss: roughly 27.0 points of it trace to shooting variance (both sides' effective field-goal rates against a .500 reference) and 25.4 to process — so the dominant reason is shooting variance (luck). On the scoring environment we projected 176.4 total points and the game produced 145, a 31.4-point miss. This grade is fixed.
