The Hardwood projects every WNBA game, player, and season — and shows its work. Every number we publish comes with its inputs visible: how much is team quality, how much is the schedule, how much is who is playing tonight. Every projection is locked before tip-off, timestamped, and graded the next morning against what happened and against the betting market's closing price — the strongest public benchmark that exists. When we beat it, the scoreboard says so. When we miss, the Error Map says exactly where and what we learned. Basketball is predictable; the market proves it nightly by pricing every game. Our job is to be closer than that price, in public, game after game — and to build the kind of analysis a front office could run on. That is the whole site. No picks-tout mystery, no black box: the number, the inputs, the grade.

Glossary

The eight terms the rest of the site assumes.

The Call
Our locked pre-tip projection — spread, total, win chance.
Line Impact
How many points a player moves the spread at her projected minutes.
Deserved record
The record a team's schedule-adjusted play has earned, coin flips removed.
Leverage
How much a game can swing the playoff picture, measured in probability.
Coverage
How often results land inside our stated range — said 90, should be 90.
Beat rate
The share of games our call was closer than the closing line.
Confirmed vs projected
Whether a lineup is announced, or our model's expectation.
The breakdown
The visible inputs that sum exactly to every call.

Every one of these is measured on a page you can open: our accuracy grades the calls, the Error Map says where we missed, and Learn walks through the reasoning from the ground up.

The Hardwood is an independent WNBA prediction and simulation project. Not affiliated with the WNBA. Every projection on this site is locked before tip-off and graded the next morning — the record is public.
