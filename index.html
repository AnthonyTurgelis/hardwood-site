How to read our call

The number is our projected margin - positive means the home team by that many.

The gold tick marks that call on the line; the teal band is the honest range around it. Basketball is noisy - a single game carries a lot of luck, so we show the spread of outcomes, not false precision.

The bar is the home team's win chance from a million simulated games. Amber notes flag who's out or questionable - availability drives more of the result than any stat.

The record

We grade every call the morning after and publish the running record - hits, misses, and what we learned. See our accuracy or read the daily review.
